# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Experience AI Runtime to be welcoming and respectful. Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and accept constructive feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publication of private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's repository contact or security reporting options. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.
